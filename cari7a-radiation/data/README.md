# CARI-7A reference dataset

Generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py` with **CARI-7A v4.2.0** (FAA CAMI). Settings: ISO GCR model with the CARI-6 heliocentric potential (`GCR_MODEL=4`), isotropic transport (`NVCR=0`), no superposition, no vehicle shielding, monthly averages. Site: 44.26°N, 72.575°W (vertical cutoff rigidity 1.77 GV). Provenance is in `metadata.json`.

> Heliocentric potential data in the CARI install ends at **2026/09 (639 MV)**. For November 2026, CARI uses that latest value.
> To refresh: download new potentials from the FAA radiobiology site into `SOLARMOD/MV-DATES.L99`, delete `.cari_work/`, and rerun the script.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `altitude_sweep.csv` | 0–120 kft every 2 kft. Columns: `absorbed_wb` (whole-body average absorbed dose, µGy/h), `si_0p5mm` and `si_0p3mm` (absorbed dose in silicon, µGy/h), and `flux_<species>` for all 37 CARI species plus total ions (particles/cm²/s). Each has a `_sigma` column (CARI 1σ). |
| `solar_sensitivity.csv` | Dose and selected fluxes at 60/80/100 kft for solar minimum (2009/12, 2019/12), solar maximum (2014/04, 2024/10) and the launch month. |
| `solar_monthly_80kft.csv` | Monthly dose rate at 80 kft from 2019-01 to 2026-09, with the heliocentric potential. |
| `mission_*.csv` | Time series (1-minute steps or finer): altitude, depth, phase, dose rates and cumulative doses. |
| `mission_summary.csv` | Totals per mission plus film metrics. Particle hits per frame assume a 35 mm 4-perf frame and fluence × area / 2. |

|---|---|---|---|

| Solar, 80 kft for 16 h | 17.0 h | **100 ± 6 µGy** | 42 days |

\*Days of ordinary storage on the ground at 0.1 µGy/h.

- **Dose rate vs altitude** (µGy/h): sea level 0.018, 40 kft 2.8, 60 kft 5.0, 80 kft 5.6, 100 kft 5.6, 120 kft 5.6. The curve is flat above about 60 kft (the Pfotzer maximum region), so **dose is driven by time aloft, not by peak altitude**.
- **Solar cycle:** at 80 kft the rate ranges from 4.2 µGy/h (solar max 2024/10) to 7.9 µGy/h (deep solar min 2009/12). That is roughly −25% to +40% around the launch-month value.
